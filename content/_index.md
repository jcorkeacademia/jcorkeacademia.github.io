I'm a statistician drawn to problems where many decision-makers interact: drivers sharing a road, players bluffing across a table, nodes wiring themselves into a network. I recently finished my M.A. in Statistics at Columbia University, after graduating summa cum laude in Mathematics from Middlebury College.

My research sits where probability, game theory and computation meet. I've worked on coordinating autonomous vehicles in mixed traffic with Prof. Sharon Di, on how to exploit an opponent safely in poker with Shayan Ravari, and on degree assortativity in combined networks with Prof. Phil Chodrow.

Outside of research I like to build things, most recently an online version of the board game *Century: Golem Edition*, with tuned bots and a solver that hunts for the fastest possible win. I also love teaching, as a TA for Network Science and as lead math and statistics tutor at Middlebury.
