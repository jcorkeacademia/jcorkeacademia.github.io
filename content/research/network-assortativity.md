*Research assistant, Network Science · Advisor: Prof. Philip Chodrow · Middlebury College · Summer 2024 – Spring 2025*

Degree assortativity measures whether well-connected nodes tend to link to other well-connected nodes. This project asked what happens to assortativity when two networks are combined into one.

**What I did**

- Developed theoretical bounds for degree assortativity under graph union operations.
- Validated the bounds with Monte Carlo simulations in Python and NetworkX across random graph families: Erdős–Rényi, Barabási–Albert and the configuration model.
- Analyzed how assortativity behaves across different regimes, produced figures comparing empirical estimates to the theoretical bounds, and wrote a manuscript summarizing the results.
